# AXEL

AXEL is a horror roguelike with a slot-machine fight system. You return to a decaying school, and every fight is a 5x5 slot grid. Made in Godot 4.7.2 with GDScript.

## Play it

- **In your browser:** https://wabbl0.github.io/AXEL/ (browser saves are separate from desktop saves)
- **Download (Windows, macOS):** https://github.com/Wabbl0/AXEL/releases/latest

## Controls

- Move: W A S D
- Interact, advance dialogue: E, Space or Enter
- Pause, close menus: Esc
- Item wheel: Tab, Q, R or I

## What is where

- `AXEL/axel_project/scenes/` - the school rooms, menus and story
- `AXEL/axel_project/combat/` - the slot-machine fights
- `AXEL/axel_project/scripts/` - shared systems (saves, music, settings)
- `AXEL/axel_project/assets/` - art, music and sound
- `AXEL/axel_project/tools/` - test and helper scripts
- `AXEL/axel_project/plans/` - design notes
