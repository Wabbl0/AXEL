# AXEL — Background Art List (for the artist)

Every background image the game needs, what should be drawn in it, and whether it's already done. Based on the HTML prototype (`AXEL/html/game.html`) and the current scene plans (`plans/scenes/AXEL_SCENES_ARC*.md`).

Style reminder for all of these: black + red (`#ff0000`) palette, decayed/rotting school, dread and guilt mood. Not gory-scary, more "sad and wrong."

**Already drawn (6):** School Exterior, Corridor, Locker Hub, Basement Door, Cafeteria, the Map paper.
Everything else below still needs art.
