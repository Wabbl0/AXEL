## Design ideology

From a senior developer's playtest feedback (2026-10). Treat as guiding intent for all balance and onboarding work.

- Like most roguelikes, AXEL lives on the fine line between difficult and replayable.
- The difficulty curve is almost exponential: the first third of the game is quite linear and easy. The difficulty spikes only after every technique has been properly explained and understood by the player.
- A win the player cannot explain feels empty. The senior developer did not know Moon Princess-style slots, did not understand what was going on, and felt nothing when he won the first round. Every first-time mechanic (matching, points, history box, skull meter, Vice Round) must be explained at the moment it happens.
- Tutorial cards close when the player does the action they ask for, on their red X, on a click anywhere on the card, or on Esc / Backspace / Delete. `interact` (E / Space / Enter) never closes or skips a card. For 0.8s after the text finishes typing, close input is ignored (read lock); a click or Esc while typing only finishes the text.
- Two kinds of fight cards (`AxelTutorial`). **Freeze cards** (Matches, Multiplier, The Skull when a power line is found, Vice Round) freeze time at the grid moment, before the line resolves (`CombatRoot.lines_found` + `lines_gate_open`), and never time out. **Calm cards** (History, The Skull in the default order, Bets, Goal, Items) never freeze or block a spin; 6s after typing they fade on their own, but that clock only runs while a spin is playing or the fight is ending, so an idle player keeps the card. Default fight order: Spin → Matches → History → The Skull → Bets → Goal (`FIGHT_ORDER`). Only one card shows at a time, except that a bet click (+/-, Q/R) shows Bets at once, and a power line shows The Skull at once, stacked over the open card. Round Complete waits until no fight card is open (Guide does not count); fight cards still queued are dropped and show in a later fight. Regression: `tools/qa/tutorial_popup_check.gd`.
- Walk fights stay almost impossible in the first areas and only become common in the late areas.
