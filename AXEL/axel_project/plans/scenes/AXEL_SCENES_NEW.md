## Usage notes

- Every line marked **[SOURCE]** in the arc files is the exact original text from `game.html`.
- Lines marked **[REWRITE]** are cues to replace with your own version.
- Once you're happy with a scene, remove the tag — that means it's locked.
- The "What was missing" appendix is at the bottom of [AXEL_SCENES_ARC8.md](AXEL_SCENES_ARC8.md).
