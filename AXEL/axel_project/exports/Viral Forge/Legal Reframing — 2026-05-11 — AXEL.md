# Legal Reframing — 2026-05-11 — AXEL

## Core Legal Principle (re: AXEL)

Slot mechanics are not copyrightable. Cluster Pays (NetEnt, 2016), cascading reels, multiplier wilds, grid-clear → free spins, RTP math — all have been freely reimplemented across hundreds of slot products and now indie roguelites (CloverPit, Luck Be a Landlord, Slots & Daggers, Slot & Dungeons, Runeborn). Mechanics: SAFE to clone.

**What IS protectable:**

- Trademarked names ("Moon Princess", "Trinity", "Play'n GO")
- Visual identity (princesses, anime-Sailor-Moon-adjacent characters, exact symbol art, color palettes that read as Moon Princess)
- Specific audio assets
- The Trinity meter NAME ("Trinity" itself is plausibly fanciful enough for trade-dress risk in a slot context)

## Reframing — AXEL

### Concept 1: AXEL (current working title)

**SOURCE TRACKING:**

- Original Source: Moon Princess 100 by Play'n GO (2023+ franchise)
- Source URL: https://slotcatalog.com/en/slots/Moon-Princess-100
- Source Metrics: Top-tier real-money slot, frequent franchise expansions (Moon Princess Origins 2025, Stargazing 2025, Power of Love 2024, Trinity 2023, original 2017). Signals durable appeal.
- What We're Copying: Cluster pays (4+ touching), cascading avalanche on win, multiplier-wild ramp meter that accumulates within a spin, grid-clear → free spins escalation. RTP math pattern.
- What We're NOT Copying: Princess characters, anime-magical-girl aesthetic, "Moon Princess" name, "Trinity" meter naming, color palette, audio cues, exact symbol art.
- Clever Tie-In: Lean into the opposite aesthetic — gritty, grungy, post-arcade — to ride the CloverPit darker-roguelite zeitgeist instead. Marketing language: "the dopamine of a cascading slot, without the casino."
- Marketing Keywords: roguelite slot, cascading slot, no-real-money gambling, cluster pays, Balatro-like, CloverPit-like, slot roguelike

**REFRAMING:**

- Original: Moon Princess 100 (princess-themed real-money slot)
- IP Risk: Direct visual + naming overlap = HIGH if shipped as-is with princesses + "Trinity"
- Reframed: AXEL — neutral codename. Theme = abstract/industrial OR thematic biomes (see variations). Multiplier meter renamed (e.g. "Surge meter", "Overload", "Chain Coil").
- Core Appeal Retained: Cascading clusters, multiplier escalation, grid-clear payoff, free-spins climax, RTP math
- Changes Made: Theme/skin, all naming, visual symbol library, audio
- Legal Safety: HIGH (mechanics-only clone, all IP elements replaced)
- Viral Potential Lost: None — the share-worthy element is the cascade visual + multiplier, not the princess theme. Princess theme would actually HURT virality vs the CloverPit-grunge zeitgeist.
