## Top Shareability Drivers Right Now

1. **Cascade visual juice:** Long avalanche chains with escalating multiplier ARE the share-moment. Frame this as the centerpiece.
2. **Run identity:** "I beat the slot with [absurd item combo from biome X]" — needs a one-tap "share run summary" button.
3. **Moral cover:** "No real money, just the dopamine" — gives streamers/players permission to share without being gambling-promo flagged on TikTok.
4. **Near-miss outrage:** Show how close the player came to the grid-clear. Built-in clip moment.

## Viral Hook Patterns Working Now

- **Identity sharing** — "My absurd Area-3 build" (Balatro-style)
- **Challenge** — daily seed leaderboard
- **Rarity bragging** — "Got the legendary X synergy"
- **Fail comedy** — bust-out at ante 8
- **Skill/luck showcase** — max-multiplier cascade clip
- **Moral hook** — "casino mechanics without the financial harm"

## Recommended Hook for AXEL

**Primary:** Skill/luck showcase via cascade max-multiplier clip moment. Make the visual peak (chain count + multiplier ramp + grid clear) so satisfying that streamers/players cut it themselves.

**Secondary:** End-of-run shareable summary card — auto-generated PNG with biomes traversed, key items, final score, seed. One-click share to Twitter/Discord. (Balatro lacks this; AXEL can win on it.)

**Tertiary:** Daily seed mode with global leaderboard — competitive identity loop.

## What NOT to Lean On

- Real-money or "spend to play more" framing → TikTok bans it, kills organic reach
- Princess/Sailor-Moon aesthetic → IP risk + crowded vs CloverPit's darker aesthetic
- Generic "casino simulator" framing → invisible vs incumbents
