## Variation 1.1 — AXEL: Grease (Industrial / Grunge)

- Mechanic: Core cluster-cascade slot with biome runs
- Visual: Pixel art, dark palette, neon accents — sits next to CloverPit on Steam wishlist pages
- Biomes: Boiler Room → Pawnshop Basement → Derelict Casino → The Vault
- Meter rename: Overload Coil
- Items: Greasy charms, soldering tools, "rigged" symbols
- Hook: "The slot you can beat. No coins required." Cascade max-multiplier = "Coil Overload" — full-screen visual moment
- Why it wins: Rides exact CloverPit/KEEP-GAMBLING zeitgeist. Highest "vibes-fit" with current trend.

## Variation 1.2 — AXEL: Resonance (Cosmic / Neon)

- Mechanic: Same
- Visual: Vector + glow + particle — fast to build, scales well, no art-team dependency
- Biomes: Nebula → Quasar → Void → Supercluster
- Meter rename: Resonance Field
- Items: Stellar fragments, harmonic crystals, wormhole shards
- Hook: Cascade payoff framed as "supernova" — extremely streamable visual
- Why it wins: Best buildability for solo dev. Vector graphics scale. Differentiated visual lane vs grunge-heavy competitors.

## Variation 1.3 — AXEL: Sigil (Fantasy Mythic)

- Mechanic: Same + light combat layer (boss at end of biome eats your cascade payout)
- Visual: Pixel-fantasy
- Biomes: Forge → Crypt → Sanctum → Throne
- Meter rename: Sigil Surge
- Items: Runes, relics, blessed coins
- Hook: Build-narrative — "I beat the Crypt boss with a triple-rune surge"
- Why it loses some: Crowded vs Slots & Daggers, Runeborn, Slot & Dungeons. Saturation risk.

## Variation 1.4 — AXEL: Tilt (Heist / Anti-Casino)

- Mechanic: Same + "heat" mechanic (each cascade adds heat; bust if you hit max)
- Visual: Vector + photo-collage, noir palette, neon casino signage
- Biomes: Strip Mall Casino → Backroom → Penthouse → Sky Vault
- Meter rename: Pressure Gauge
- Items: Lockpicks, signal jammers, marked symbols
- Hook: Strongest moral/narrative framing ("rigging the slot that rigs you") — TikTok/discourse-friendly
- Why it wins: Strongest distinct narrative angle. Cuts through alongside CloverPit rather than competing on aesthetic.

## Variation 1.5 — AXEL: ZERO (Emoji-Only Prototype)

- Mechanic: Core only, no biome system — minimal viable cascade slot roguelite
- Visual: Emoji symbols on CSS grid, web-only
- Biomes: None (single run, escalating ante like Balatro)
- Meter rename: Chain Charge
- Items: ~12 simple symbol-modifying items
- Hook: Daily seed + auto-generated PNG run summary share card
- Why it wins: Ship in 1–2 weeks to validate K-factor before committing to the full AXEL build. Web/mobile-shareable URL = instant TikTok-friendly. Doubles as marketing channel for full AXEL.
